# Architecture rules
- Keep source-based news articles in dedicated pages, with shared article metadata consumed by the news overview and SEO route catalogue to avoid date and URL drift.
- Share actionable service checklists between the managed-services hub and maintenance detail page; retain both existing URLs while consolidating navigation under the hub to preserve inbound links.
- Keep the approved Whitepaper as a static public download for the external FTP deployment; Lovable-relative asset pointers alone are not served by Serverprofis.
